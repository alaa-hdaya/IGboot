# GostProcess: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 13 October 2026_

GostProcess ("we", "us") provides AI automation and workflow services to businesses. This policy explains how we handle personal data when you contact us through our Instagram account and when our AI assistant answers your direct messages.

## 1. What data we collect
When you send a direct message to our Instagram account, we receive through Meta's official Instagram Messaging API:
- your Instagram-scoped user ID and, when available, your Instagram username;
- the content of the messages you send us and our replies;
- the date and time of the messages.

From the conversation we may note business information you choose to share (for example your type of business, the process you want to automate, the tools you use, and whether you would like a consultation).

We do not ask for passwords, payment details, or identity documents in Instagram messages.

## 2. Why we use it
- To answer your questions about our services.
- To understand your needs and offer a consultation if you are interested.
- To let a member of our team follow up with you when needed.

## 3. AI assistant
Our replies may be generated by an AI assistant. Message content is processed by our AI provider only to generate the reply. You can ask at any time to talk to a human team member.

## 4. Where data is stored and who can access it
Conversation data is stored in our internal tools (our workflow automation platform and a private spreadsheet), accessible only to the GostProcess team. We do not sell your data and do not share it with third parties for advertising.

## 5. How long we keep it
Message history is kept for up to 90 days. Basic lead information (business type, interest, status) is kept as long as needed to follow up on your request, then deleted.

## 6. Your rights and data deletion
You can ask us at any time to access, correct, or delete your data.
**To request deletion:** send us a direct message on Instagram saying "delete my data", or email us at [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL]. We delete your messages and lead information within 30 days and confirm by reply.
